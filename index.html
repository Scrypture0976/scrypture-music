ABOUT SCRYPTURE

Scrypture makes music for Yah's lost sheep. The name carries the mission: we are only set free and made whole by keeping Yahuah's commandments. As John 8:32 declares, “You shall know the truth, and the truth shall make you free.” Every song is built on the words of Yah — a wake-up call from slumber, an invitation to return to Yahuah and serve Him with a repented heart. With a growing catalog including “I Can't Give Up,” “King Of Israel,” “How Do I?,” and “Prayer,” Scrypture meets listeners in their struggle and points them to the hope that makes us whole.
